Installation

Back up settings, establish a stock baseline and arrange a device-specific recovery route first. Previous TAP rollback results do not guarantee recovery. Read the hardware preparation guide.

  1. Keep the downloaded MXPLUS_MAINLINE_UNTESTED.bin and its receipt. Make a separate copy named MX-1_UPD.BIN for installation. Keep the stock file elsewhere.
  2. With the MX-1 off, hold TAP while switching it on. Connect the computer to USB-B and open the MX-1 drive.
  3. Copy only the prepared MX-1_UPD.BIN to the drive’s top level. Wait for copying to finish, eject the drive, then disconnect USB.
  4. Press TAP to update. Keep power connected until Fin appears, then switch off. If the expected sequence fails, use the established recovery plan.
  5. Restart with monitoring muted and follow the bench checks. The displayed Roland version does not identify mxplus.

Transfer steps follow Roland’s update procedure. The renamed experimental file is an mxplus step, not a Roland-supported update.
