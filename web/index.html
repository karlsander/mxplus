Installation

  1. Back up data. With the MX-1 off, hold pad 1 while switching it on. Connect USB-B to the computer and open the MX-1 drive. Copy all 95 files from its BACKUP folder into a folder on the computer. These contain MIX, MASTER FX and MASTER FX COMBI settings. Eject the drive, disconnect USB, then switch off.
  2. Rename the downloaded file from MX1_UPD_mxplus.bin to MX-1_UPD.BIN, and keep a copy of the original Roland file in another folder.
  3. Enter update mode as described in Roland’s update procedure: hold TAP while switching on the MX-1. Connect USB-B to the computer and open the MX-1 drive.
  4. Copy MX-1_UPD.BIN to the drive, safely eject the drive, then disconnect USB.
  5. Press TAP to update. Keep power connected until Fin appears on the left display, then switch off.
  6. Restart with your speakers or headphones turned off or unplugged and the mixer volume turned down. Then slowly turn up the volume and check mixer operation.
